Position 6 of the hemoglobin β-chain. Glutamate (E) is negatively charged and sits on the protein surface. Valine (V) is hydrophobic — it sticks to neighbouring molecules, causing hemoglobin to polymerize into rigid fibers. One character change. The cell's shape, and its ability to carry oxygen, completely breaks down.
